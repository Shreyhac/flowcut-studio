# Results and clients

## @shreyansharora05: the studio's own channel
An Instagram channel about AI and developer tools, edited end to end with Reel Factory.

Channel audit (Aug 2026, 35 reels, public counts):

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total plays | **401,315** |
| Best reel | **143,621** plays, 2,186 likes, 1,034 comments ("goldfish memory: every session is a first date with your codebase") |
| Second best | **128,305** plays, 1,924 likes, 561 comments ("token burn: $40 deep by 9 am") |
| Other breakouts | 20,488 ("Claude builds ugly websites", 239 comments) · 20,097 (model/price index chart) |
| Median reel | 2,874 plays, about 2× the follower count at the time |

**What the data taught the pipeline:**
- The breakouts were the longest tech posts (49–51 s).
- They personified the tool as a "bad employee": a complaint the viewer agrees with and sends to a colleague, not a tutorial.
- They had **no lead-magnet CTA**.
- Career/jobs content and "here's a cool tool" demos without a pain point were the dead lanes.

These findings are written into the editor's learnings and drive hook selection.
